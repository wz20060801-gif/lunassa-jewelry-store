# LUNASSA Image Replacement Guide

Current pages use CSS-generated placeholder visuals so the project can run without real product photos.

Recommended replacement sizes:

- Homepage hero model image: 1920 × 1080 or 1800 × 1100, JPG/WebP, horizontal luxury campaign photo.
- Product card image: 1200 × 1200, JPG/WebP, square transparent/clean background.
- Product detail gallery main image: 1200 × 1500 or 1600 × 2000, vertical close-up.
- Collection banner / inserted brand image: 1800 × 800, horizontal lifestyle/series campaign photo.
- Mobile detail image: keep important jewelry in center 70% area.

Suggested visual direction:

- Background: ivory, warm gray, jade green, champagne gold.
- Model: collarbone / hand / ear close-up, no busy props.
- Product lighting: soft natural light, premium jewelry photography, clear metal and stone details.
- Avoid: red promotion graphics, crowded Taobao-style text, excessive discount badges.
